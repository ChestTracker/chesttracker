Questions

Can I get banned?

ChestTracker only reads the replay files that Fortnite saves on your PC, the same files the in-game replay viewer uses. It doesn't read the game's memory, doesn't change the game and doesn't use your Epic account.

Does it show chests live during the match?

The game unlocks the replay only when the match ends, so your chests appear about 2 seconds after the match. The in-game card then shows everything.

Can I use it on another PC?

One key works on one PC at a time. To move it: Settings → Licence → Deactivate this PC, then activate it on the new one.

Which modes are counted?

Battle Royale, Build and Zero Build: Solo, Duos, Trios and Squads. Other modes are ignored and the overlay hides itself.

Windows says "unknown app"

That's normal for new apps from small developers: click "More info" → "Run anyway".
