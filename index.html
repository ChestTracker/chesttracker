FAQ

Can I use it on another PC?

One key works on one PC at a time. To move it, open Settings → Licence → Deactivate this PC, then activate it on the new PC.

Does it show chests live during the match?

No. The game unlocks the replay only when the match ends, so chests are counted about 2 seconds after the match.

Can I get banned?

ChestTracker only reads replay files that the game saves on your PC, the same files the in-game replay viewer uses. It does not read or change the game.

Windows says "unknown app"

That's normal for new apps from small developers: click "More info" → "Run anyway".
